About

This platform provides technical traders and analysts with automatically detected stock wedge patterns based on historical market data. The goal is simple: cut through the noise and present clean, actionable setups driven by repeatable technical conditions.

Whether you're scanning for breakouts or monitoring volume trends, this tool offers a quick snapshot of potential trading opportunities.
